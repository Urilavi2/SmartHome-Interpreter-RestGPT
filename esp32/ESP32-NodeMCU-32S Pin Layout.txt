ESP32-C6 Pin Layout
----------------------

GND - LCD
5V - LCD
LINE 14 = PIN22(downside) = PIN36(upside): LCD SCL,PURPLE
LINE 14 = PIN21(downside) = PIN33(upside): LCD SDA, BLUE 
I2C address at 0x27
https://lastminuteengineers.com/esp32-i2c-lcd-tutorial/

LINE16 = P34: Potentiometer 

LINE 20 = P25: Red
LINE 21 = P26: Green
LINE 22 = P27: Blue

 
LINE 22 = P35: Temperature sensor
